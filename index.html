Why This App Exists

This app was designed for massive speaking practice in English. It is not a test, does not assign grades, and is not meant to judge performance. The goal is personal, high-volume repetition using frequent phrases and vocabulary from real social contexts.

What You Have Here

A practical training app focused on speaking volume and consistency.

🎮 How to Use This Screen

  • Choose Theme, Study Type, Topic and Level.
  • Click Start Practice to open the sentence cards.
  • Use Previous and Next to move through phrases.
  • Repeat the same sentence several times before moving to the next one. With time, speaking will feel more natural and comfortable.
  • Use Random to jump to a surprise phrase.
  • Use EN/PT to switch the instructions language.
  • Use Back to Menu to return to this screen.

🗣️ Pronunciation Practice

11 pronunciation techniques with 3 difficulty levels:

  • Word Stress Patterns - emphasis practice (BREAKfast, toMOrrow)
  • Linking Sounds - connected speech (check_in, wake_up)
  • Contractions Practice - contractions with IPA (I'm -> /aɪm/)
  • Silent Letters - highlighted silent letters (k(nife), w(rite))
  • Over-Articulation - exaggerated articulation for muscle training
  • Tongue Twisters - fluency drills
  • Minimal Pairs Training - similar sounds (ship/sheep) with IPA
  • Chunking (Natural Blocks) - natural speech blocks
  • Stress and Intonation - key word emphasis (bold markers)
  • Thought Groups - idea groups with pauses (/)
  • Reading Practice - reading flow with pause marks
